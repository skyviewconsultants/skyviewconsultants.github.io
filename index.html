About Us

Free guidance, clear options and personal support

Sky View Consultants supports students who want to study in the UK or continue their education after completing a qualification.

We help you understand your study options, compare suitable university routes, prepare applications and plan the next step.

Our consultancy is free. Students can start by telling us about their academic background, preferred subject and future plans.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the consultancy free?

Yes. Sky View Consultants promotes free initial study consultancy for students exploring UK study options.

Can you help students already in the UK?

Yes. Students already in the UK can discuss further study, another Master's, doctoral routes and application planning.

Can I use the Course Finder as a final course list?

No. It is an initial exploration tool. Current course availability, fees, entry requirements and intakes should be confirmed before applying.

How do I apply?

Use the Apply Now option or complete the consultation form. Your enquiry will be prepared for WhatsApp.
